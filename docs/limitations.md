# Limitations

Pricing tables may become stale; calculated cost may differ from invoices; provider list price may differ from an enterprise rate; local inference estimates depend on supplied assumptions and are not electricity bills; infrastructure allocation may be approximate; unknown costs and outcomes make aggregates incomplete; provider token semantics differ; estimated tokens are not provider-reported tokens; outcome quality depends on correct instrumentation; cost per success depends on outcome definitions; projected savings are not realized savings; forecasts are simple deterministic projections; anomaly detection is rule-based; currency conversion requires supplied rates; SQLite is for local use, not a large warehouse; adapters are portable local transformations rather than verified live integrations; OpenTelemetry mapping may be incomplete; FOCUS export is experimental unless formally verified; AIMeter does not enforce budgets itself; schema and APIs may change before 1.0.
